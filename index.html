Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our fast, private, and 100% in-browser utilities.

How do these tools process files and text without server uploads?

All 109 utilities run 100% locally in your web browser using modern Web APIs, JavaScript, WebAssembly, and HTML5 Canvas. Your documents, photos, audio clips, and data never leave your device.

Are all calculators, converters, and PDF tools completely free to use?

Yes. Every single tool on Free Online Tools is 100% free with unlimited daily usage, no hidden paywalls, no credit card requirements, and no account sign-up needed.

Can I use these online tools on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes, all tools are built with responsive, mobile-first design and touch-friendly controls. They work seamlessly across iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux devices in any modern web browser.

Is my sensitive personal or business data kept private and secure?

Absolutely. Because processing happens strictly inside your browser's sandboxed local memory with zero server transmission, your sensitive financial metrics, client PDFs, passwords, and reseller numbers remain completely private to you.
