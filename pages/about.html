    • About Nexivo

    A privacy-first suite of 100+ specialized calculators and fast browser utilities designed for everyday productivity—free for creators, students, and professionals without tracking, paywalls, or forced sign-ups.

    Our Mission & Purpose

    Nexivo (https://nexivo.com) was created to simplify everyday math, technical workflows, and content creation tasks through instant, private, and accessible browser-based tools.

    Too many online utilities demand account creation, hide basic features behind paywalls, or upload your sensitive documents, financial estimates, and client data to remote servers. At Nexivo, every calculation and file transformation takes place directly inside your own web browser. Your inputs stay on your device, ensuring maximum privacy and zero latency.

    100+ Specialized Calculators & Daily Utilities

    Our comprehensive directory features over 100 high-accuracy utilities tailored to creators, makers, freelancers, and small business owners:

    • Specialized Maker & Industry Calculators: Plan and quote projects accurately with our Solar Panel ROI & Battery Bank Sizer, 3D Print Filament Cost & Time Estimator, LTL Freight Density & Class Calculator, Fabric Yardage & Sewing Seam Allowance Estimator, Sourdough Hydration & Baker's Percentage Calculator, and Reseller Profit & Fee Calculator (supporting Poshmark, Mercari, eBay, Depop, and Vinted).
    • Business, Creator & Social Tools: Newsletter Sponsorship & CPM Rate Calculator, YouTube Ad Revenue Estimator, WhatsApp & PayPal Link Generators, and JSON-LD Google Rich Snippet Schema Generators.
    • Everyday Math & Academic Calculators: Percentage & Discount Calculator, Grade & GPA Calculator, Universal Unit Converter, Age Difference & Birthday Countdown tools.
    • Document & PDF Productivity: Merge, split, rotate, extract pages, count pages, watermark, and digitally sign PDF documents with zero file uploads to any external server.
    • Design & Developer Tools: CSS Bento Grid, Mesh Gradient & Glassmorphism Visual Studio, Favicon & Web Manifest Pack Generator, JSON Formatter & Validator, Strong Password Generator, Base64 Encoder/Decoder, Cryptographic Hash Generator, and EXIF Metadata Reader.

    Why Local In-Browser Processing Matters

    • Zero File Uploads & Total Privacy: When you calculate profit margins, format confidential configurations, or review documents, all data is handled purely within your browser's local memory. No files or metrics ever leave your machine.
    • Instant Real-Time Results: Calculations execute at device speeds without network round-trips, rate limiting, or server queues.
    • 100% Free Forever: Because our architecture eliminates costly backend processing clusters, we provide our entire collection of 100+ utilities completely free with unlimited daily usage.
    • No Account Required: Jump straight into your calculations with no login screens, email collection, or annoying popups.
