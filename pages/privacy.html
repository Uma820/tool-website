    • Privacy Policy

    Last Updated: November 16, 2026 | Effective Date: November 2026

    1. Verified Client-Side Data Architecture

    At Nexivo (https://nexivo.com), privacy is implemented through our software architecture rather than through vague legal promises. The 100+ utilities available on this site—including calculators, text formatters, code utilities, image converters, and PDF tools—execute entirely within your browser's local JavaScript runtime.

    Your tool inputs and files are not uploaded to our web servers. Text strings, passwords, numbers, and uploaded files reside exclusively in your device's volatile memory (RAM) for the duration of your session. When you refresh or close the tab, that data is automatically discarded by your browser.

    2. Browser Local Storage Disclosures

    This website uses the standard browser localStorage API for a single functional preference:

    • fot_user_theme: Stores your visual interface choice ("light" or "dark") so your preferred theme persists across visits.

    No personal information, IP addresses, search queries, or submitted tool data are ever stored in localStorage. You can delete this stored preference at any time using your browser's "Clear Site Data" feature.

    3. Web Server Access Logs

    While our application scripts collect no user data, standard web server infrastructure automatically records basic HTTP request logs when delivering static web assets. When your browser requests any page, stylesheet, or asset, the server records:

    • Your public IP address.
    • Date and time of the request.
    • Requested URI path and HTTP status code.
    • HTTP Referrer header (the page that linked you to the requested file).
    • User-Agent string (browser type, operating system, and version).

    These logs are maintained directly by the hosting infrastructure for technical diagnostics, performance monitoring, and defense against malicious network activity. They are not linked to any personal profiles.

    4. Cookies and Advertising

    Nexivo uses cookies and local storage to deliver, improve, and protect our services, as well as to support site operations.

    Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to Nexivo (https://nexivo.com) and other websites across the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

    Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

    • Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
    • Network Advertising Initiative & DAA Opt-Out: https://www.aboutads.info

    Alternatively, you can choose to disable or selectively turn off third-party cookies in your browser settings.

    5. Self-Hosted Client-Side Libraries

    To protect user privacy and prevent data leakage through external Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), open-source helper libraries are self-hosted locally on our own servers. Loading our utilities does not trigger unauthorized tracking or external requests to third-party CDN providers.

    6. External Links

    Nexivo may contain links to external websites, documentation, and web standards for educational or navigational purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of external third-party websites. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any external sites they visit.

    7. Children's Privacy (Under Age 13)

    Nexivo is designed as a general-audience productivity and educational utility platform. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. All calculators, converters, and tools run locally in the browser with zero account registration and zero personal profile creation. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through our contact channels, please contact us immediately so we can remove the communication.
