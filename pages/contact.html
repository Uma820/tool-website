    Reporting a Bug or Issue

    All calculations and transformations on Nexivo execute client-side directly in your browser. If a tool behaves unexpectedly, please include the following details in your email to help us diagnose and resolve the issue quickly:

    • Tool URL: The page or tool you were using (e.g., /tools/reseller-profit-calculator.html).
    • Browser & Device: Your web browser and operating system (e.g., Chrome on Windows 11, Safari on iOS).
    • Sample Inputs: The numbers or sample input text that caused the unexpected behavior (never send private passwords or personal sensitive data).
    • Observed vs. Expected Result: A brief description of what happened compared to what you anticipated.
