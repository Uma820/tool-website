    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The Text to Slug Converter transforms article titles, product names, and document headings into lowercase, URL-safe slugs. It strips special characters, transliterates accented Latin vowels, and separates words with clean hyphens or underscores.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Type or paste your headline or phrase into the input field.
    2. Select your preferred separator: Hyphen (-) or Underscore (_).
    3. The clean URL slug updates instantly.
    4. Click "Copy Slug" to copy it to your clipboard for use in your CMS or Git branch.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • Blog & CMS URL Structure: Create readable, SEO-optimized permalinks for WordPress, Ghost, or static site generators.
    • Git Branch Naming: Turn feature descriptions like "Fix user authentication bug" into valid branch names like fix-user-authentication-bug.
    • Web Asset & Image Naming: Convert spaces and illegal symbols in photograph titles into web-safe filenames.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Decomposes Unicode characters via normalize('NFD') and removes diacritical marks via /[\u0300-\u036f]/g. Replaces ampersands (&) with "and", removes illegal symbols, collapses consecutive whitespace, and replaces delimiters.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Non-Latin scripts (such as Arabic, Bengali, or Hindi) without direct phonetic Latin transliteration equivalents are either preserved in URL-safe format or stripped depending on standard ASCII slug settings.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    100% client-side memory execution.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why are hyphens preferred over underscores for URLs?

    Major search engines like Google treat hyphens as word separators in URLs, whereas underscores may be treated as joiners.

    How are accented letters like é and ü handled?

    They are automatically converted into their basic Latin equivalents (e and u).

    Does it remove punctuation like question marks and colons?

    Yes. All non-alphanumeric punctuation marks are stripped to ensure URL validity.
