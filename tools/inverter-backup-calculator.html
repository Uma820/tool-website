    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The Inverter Backup Calculator helps homeowners, small businesses, and solar technicians size the two most critical components of an emergency backup power system: the Inverter Capacity (rated in Volt-Amps / VA) and the Battery Bank Capacity (rated in Amp-Hours / Ah).

    Undersizing an inverter causes overload beeps, thermal tripping, and failure to start motorized appliances like fans and refrigerators. Conversely, undersizing the battery bank means your lights cut out after only 45 minutes instead of the desired 4 to 6 hours. This tool uses professional electrical standards—incorporating power factors ($0.8\text{ PF}$), a $25\%$ safety headroom multiplier, and Depth of Discharge limits—to recommend commercial hardware configurations that guarantee dependable runtime.

    Mathematical Formulas Used

    Sizing calculations conform to modern IEEE and NEC standards:

    • Total Running Load ($P_{\text{load}}$):
      $$P_{\text{load (Watts)}} = \sum (\text{Wattage}_i \times \text{Quantity}_i)$$
    • Recommended Inverter Rating ($\text{VA}_{\text{inverter}}$):
      $$\text{VA} = \left( \frac{P_{\text{load}}}{\text{Power Factor (0.8)}} \right) \times 1.25 \quad (\text{safety headroom})$$ (Rounded up to nearest commercial rating: 700VA, 900VA, 1100VA, 1500VA, 2kVA, 3kVA, etc.)
    • Total Energy Needed from Battery ($E_{\text{battery}}$):
      $$E_{\text{battery (Wh)}} = \frac{P_{\text{load}} \times T_{\text{hours}}}{\eta_{\text{inverter efficiency (0.85)}}}$$
    • Required Battery Bank Capacity ($C_{\text{Ah}}$):
      $$C_{\text{Ah}} = \frac{E_{\text{battery (Wh)}}}{V_{\text{system}} \times (\text{DoD \%} / 100)}$$

    • Apartment Inverter Sizing: Sizing an inverter to run 3 ceiling fans, 5 LED lights, a TV, and Wi-Fi during 3-hour evening blackouts.
    • Pure Sine Wave vs. Square Wave Decision: Verifying whether your load contains delicate electronics (laptops, TVs) or motors (refrigerators, fans) that strictly require Pure Sine Wave inverters to avoid buzzing and motor overheating.
    • Commercial Shop Backup: Sizing battery storage to keep point-of-sale (POS) terminals, security cameras, and LED lighting active during daytime grid cuts.

    Realistic Example & Expected Output

    Scenario: You want 4 hours of backup for: 3 Ceiling Fans ($3 \times 75\text{W} = 225\text{W}$), 5 LED Bulbs ($5 \times 12\text{W} = 60\text{W}$), 1 TV ($100\text{W}$), and 1 Wi-Fi router ($15\text{W}$) = 400W total running load on a 12V system with Tubular Lead-Acid (50% DoD).

    • Inverter VA Rating: $(400 / 0.8) \times 1.25 = 625\text{ VA} \implies \mathbf{700\text{ VA}}$ or $\mathbf{900\text{ VA}}$ commercial rating.
    • Energy Required: $(400\text{W} \times 4\text{h}) / 0.85\text{ eff} = 1,882\text{ Wh}$.
    • Battery Bank Capacity: $\frac{1,882\text{ Wh}}{12\text{V} \times 0.50} = \mathbf{313.7\text{ Ah}}$.
    • Hardware Recommendation: Two 12V 160Ah batteries connected in parallel (or upgrade to a 24V system with two 150Ah batteries in series).

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a Pure Sine Wave inverter and do I need one?

    Yes, modern households should always choose Pure Sine Wave inverters. Cheaper "Modified Sine Wave" or square wave inverters cause ceiling fans to emit an annoying humming noise, cause motors to run hot, and can damage sensitive electronics like televisions and laptops.

    Can I run an air conditioner on a home inverter?

    Yes, but it requires a large system. A 1.5 Ton Inverter AC draws roughly 1,500W continuously. Running it for 4 hours requires a 48V inverter rated at least 3.5 kVA and approximately 400Ah of battery capacity.

    How many years does an inverter battery last?

    Standard flooded tubular lead-acid batteries last 3 to 5 years when maintained with distilled water and kept above 50% DoD. Modern LiFePO4 lithium batteries last 8 to 12 years (3,500+ cycles) with zero maintenance.
