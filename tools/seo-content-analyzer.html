    What Does the SEO Content Analyzer Do?

    The SEO Content Analyzer & On-Page Auditor inspects articles, blog posts, and HTML markup against established on-page search engine optimization guidelines. Rather than relying on black-box external scrapers or paying for expensive enterprise subscriptions, you can paste your text or HTML to evaluate title tags, heading structure (H1–H6), word counts, accessibility image alt attributes, and keyword density.

    It also calculates the standard Flesch Reading Ease score, allowing editors to balance search relevance with smooth human readability and clear sentence structure.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Input Article or HTML: Paste your written article, Google Doc text, or exported HTML source code into the main text area, or click Sample HTML Article.
    2. Review the SEO Health Score: Check the overall score and the actionable checklist to see what elements passed, what needs attention, and what critical errors exist (such as missing H1 tags or absent image alt attributes).
    3. Evaluate Readability: Verify your Flesch Reading Ease score (scores between 60 and 70 represent plain English accessible to general audiences).
    4. Inspect Keyword Density: Review the top recurring keywords to avoid keyword stuffing penalties (keep primary phrase density below 3.5%).

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • DOM Parsing: Uses native browser DOMParser to safely parse HTML structures into an isolated document tree.
    • Readability Formula: Implements the Flesch Reading Ease algorithm: 206.835 - 1.015 × (words / sentences) - 84.6 × (syllables / words).
    • Tokenization: Filters common English stop words ("the", "and", "is", "of") to surface genuine thematic keywords and n-grams.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Due to browser security and Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) protections, client-side web tools cannot fetch and scrape arbitrary third-party web addresses directly. You must copy and paste your HTML or content into the analyzer.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why can't I enter a URL to analyze a live webpage directly?

    Modern web browsers block client-side JavaScript from fetching arbitrary external websites to prevent cross-site scripting (CORS policies). To audit an existing webpage, right-click the page, choose "View Page Source", and paste the HTML directly into this tool.

    Why is having only one H1 tag important?

    The <h1> tag communicates the overarching topic of a page to search engines and screen readers. Having zero H1s obscures the main topic, while multiple H1s can dilute topical focus.

    What is a good Flesch Reading Ease score?

    Scores between 60 and 70 are considered standard for consumer web content (easily understood by 13- to 15-year-olds). Higher scores (70–90) are very easy, while scores below 50 reflect academic or technical prose.
