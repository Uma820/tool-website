    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The Baker's Percentage Calculator bridges the gap between home cooking measurements and professional bakery formulas. In standard culinary recipes, proportions are hidden because weights are listed in cups or grams that don't easily reveal ratios. Baker's percentages treat the combined weight of all flours as the absolute $100\%$ anchor point. This unlocks the ability to instantly assess dough hydration, adjust salt-to-flour ratios, scale production up or down, and troubleshoot dough texture.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Select your calculation direction: Use Mode 1 (Weights → Baker's %) to discover the percentages of an existing recipe, or Mode 2 (Baker's % → Weights) to generate ingredient weights for a custom batch size.
    2. Input or adjust flours: Add your flours (Bread Flour, Whole Wheat, Rye). Their combined weight automatically defines the $100\%$ baseline.
    3. Enter liquids & seasonings: Add water, salt, yeast, starter, fats (butter/oil), and sugar.
    4. Inspect the Baker's Percentage table: Observe individual baker's percentages, total batch dough weight, and overall recipe hydration.
    5. Copy the formula: Click "Copy Formatted Formula" to preserve a standard baker's sheet with percentages and weights.

    Mathematical Formulas Used

    Baker's Math Core Equations:

    Total Flour Weight = Flour 1 + Flour 2 + ... + Flour N  (Anchor = 100%)
Ingredient Baker's % = (Ingredient Weight / Total Flour Weight) × 100
Overall Hydration % = (Total Water Weight / Total Flour Weight) × 100
Target Ingredient Weight = Target Flour Weight × (Ingredient Baker's % / 100)
Base Flour from Dough Weight = Target Dough Weight / (Total Baker's % / 100)

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    The system maintains exact floating-point precision for internal conversions and applies conditional rounding: values under $25\text{g}$ display with tenths of a gram ($0.1\text{g}$) precision, while larger weights display as clean whole numbers.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Baker's percentages are weight-based. Volume measurements (cups, teaspoons) vary drastically depending on packing density (e.g. 1 cup of sifted flour weighs ~120g, whereas 1 cup of scooped flour can weigh 150g). For best results, use a digital kitchen scale.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is salt almost always 2% in Baker's percentages?

    Two percent ($2.0\%$) salt is the international bakery gold standard. It provides optimal seasoning, regulates enzymatic yeast activity, and strengthens gluten bonds without overpowering wheat flavor.

    How do I handle eggs or milk in Baker's percentage calculations?

    Liquid enrichments can be entered as their own ingredient rows. Whole eggs consist of approximately 75% water and 25% fats/protein; cow's milk is roughly 88% water.

    What does "DDT" (Desired Dough Temperature) mean?

    DDT is the target dough temperature right after mixing (typically 24°C–26°C / 76°F–78°F). Bakers calculate water temperature by subtracting room temp, flour temp, and friction factor from the target temperature.
