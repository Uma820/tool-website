    What Does the Open Graph Previewer Do?

    The Open Graph & Social Card Previewer simulates the rich interactive media cards generated when links to your website are posted on major social and messaging networks including Facebook, Twitter / X, LinkedIn, and Discord.

    Social networks crawl pages specifically looking for Open Graph (og:*) and Twitter (twitter:*) meta tags. If your tags are missing, poorly proportioned, or truncated, links will display as plain unstyled text or awkward low-resolution thumbnails. This tool lets you preview cards locally and even upload draft images directly from your computer to inspect proportions before pushing live.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Enter Social Metadata: Type your target title, description, and webpage URL into the input fields, or click Sample Data.
    2. Set Social Image: Paste a live image URL or click Test Local Image File to load any PNG or JPG from your computer without uploading it to an external server.
    3. Switch Platforms: Click between Facebook, Twitter / X, LinkedIn, and Discord tabs to verify formatting across different themes, fonts, and container widths.
    4. Verify Aspect Ratio: Ensure critical branding or typography in your image sits within the 1.91:1 safe aspect ratio and isn't cropped at the top or bottom.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • Aspect Ratios: Standard 1.91:1 ratio (1200 × 630 px) with automatic CSS object-fit: cover cropping emulation.
    • Local Testing: Employs native URL.createObjectURL() for immediate zero-upload testing of local artwork.
    • Styling Fidelity: Accurate font families, line clamps, and border radii matching each social network's official web interfaces.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Social networks cache link previews aggressively (often for 7 to 30 days). If you change an Open Graph tag on a live website, you must usually use the network's official scraper tool (such as Facebook Sharing Debugger, Twitter Card Validator, or LinkedIn Post Inspector) to clear their server cache.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    All image rendering and card previews occur strictly within your browser. If you select a local graphic from your computer, it is loaded into memory via an in-browser blob URL and is never transmitted over the internet.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the recommended size for Open Graph images?

    The golden standard is 1200 × 630 pixels with an aspect ratio of 1.91:1. Keep text centered within a 1200 × 600 area to prevent edge clipping on mobile feeds.

    Why does Twitter show a small square thumbnail instead of a large card?

    This happens if you omit <meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">. By default, Twitter defaults to a compact summary card with a small square thumbnail.

    Can I test images that aren't on a live web server yet?

    Yes! Use the "Test Local Image File" file picker to load any PNG, JPG, or WebP file directly from your computer.
