    What Does the Image to PDF Converter Do?

    The Image to PDF Converter compiles multiple image files (JPG, PNG, and WebP) into a single, cohesive PDF document. Rather than sending individual image attachments that clutter email inboxes, this tool merges your receipts, photographic scans, artwork, and presentation slides into a professional document ready for sharing, archiving, or printing.

    Using client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript via pdf-lib, your photos are processed directly in your device's memory. Your files are never uploaded to a cloud server, ensuring absolute privacy for sensitive financial documents, passports, and IDs.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Select or Drop Images: Drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP files into the designated dropzone, or click to browse.
    2. Reorder Pages: Use the left (←) and right (→) arrows on each image card to arrange your pages into the desired sequence.
    3. Choose Page Layout: Select Fit to Image to maintain original photo dimensions, or choose Standard A4 / US Letter with optional margins for formal printing.
    4. Convert & Save: Click Convert & Download PDF. The compiled document will be generated in your browser and automatically downloaded.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • PDF Processing Core: Powered by the open-source pdf-lib library (MIT License) running 100% client-side.
    • Image Format Handling: Directly embeds native JPEG and PNG streams without quality loss. WebP images are dynamically converted via Canvas before embedding.
    • Output Standard: Generates ISO 32000-1 compliant PDF 1.7 documents compatible with Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, and modern web browsers.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Because all images are held in your browser's active tab memory during document generation, compiling hundreds of 20+ megapixel photos simultaneously may strain devices with low RAM. For optimal performance, compiling batches of 1 to 50 photos is recommended.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    Your photos, confidential paperwork, and personal documents never leave your computer. All rendering, resizing, and PDF compiling happen completely inside your web browser. No files are ever uploaded to an external server.
