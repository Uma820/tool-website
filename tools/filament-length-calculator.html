    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The Filament Length Calculator performs high-precision, two-way mathematical conversions between 3D printing filament weight in grams and extrusion length in meters or feet.

    Filament is universally sold and purchased by weight (e.g., 1 kg spools), but 3D printer slicers (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Cura) and G-code commands command feed rates in linear length (millimeters and meters). Because different polymers possess radically different physical densities—ABS is 16% lighter than PLA, and PLA is 5% lighter than PLA-CF—a 1 kg spool of ABS produces roughly 60 more meters of filament than PLA. This calculator ensures exact conversion between linear G-code commands and physical spool grams.

    Mathematical Formulas Used

    Filament strand conversions model filament as an continuous cylindrical rod:

    • Cross-Sectional Area ($A_{\text{filament}}$):
      $$r = \frac{\text{Diameter (mm)}}{20} \quad (\text{radius in cm})$$ $$A = \pi \times r^2 \quad (\text{cm}^2)$$ For 1.75 mm: $A = \pi \times 0.0875^2 \approx 0.0240528\text{ cm}^2$
      For 2.85 mm: $A = \pi \times 0.1425^2 \approx 0.063794\text{ cm}^2$
    • Weight to Length:
      $$V_{\text{cm}^3} = \frac{\text{Weight (g)}}{\rho_{\text{density (g/cm}^3\text{)}}}$$ $$\text{Length (m)} = \frac{V_{\text{cm}^3}}{A \times 100}$$ $$\text{Length (ft)} = \text{Length (m)} \times 3.28084$$
    • Length to Weight:
      $$V_{\text{cm}^3} = A \times (\text{Length (m)} \times 100)$$ $$\text{Weight (g)} = V_{\text{cm}^3} \times \rho_{\text{density}}$$ $$\text{Weight (oz)} = \text{Weight (g)} \times 0.035274$$

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does 2.85mm filament have so few meters per spool?

    Cross-sectional area is proportional to the square of the diameter ($r^2$). A 2.85mm cylinder has 2.65 times the cross-sectional area of a 1.75mm cylinder. Therefore, each meter of 2.85mm filament weighs 2.65 times more than a meter of 1.75mm.

    How does filament density change with carbon fiber additives?

    Milled carbon fiber fragments increase polymer mass per volume, raising PLA density from 1.24 to ~1.30 g/cm³. This slightly decreases the total meters per kilogram, but significantly improves stiffness and heat resistance.

    Can I use this tool for TPU flexible filament?

    Yes! Select TPU 95A (density 1.21 g/cm³). Although TPU stretches during extrusion, its relaxed state before entering the extruder drive gears conforms exactly to standard volumetric geometry.
