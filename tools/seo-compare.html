    What Does the SEO Content Compare Tool Do?

    The SEO Content Compare & Competitor Auditor enables authors, SEO strategists, and marketing teams to benchmark two competing articles or web pages side-by-side. By contrasting word depth, Flesch reading ease scores, heading structures (H1–H3 counts), and primary keyword targeting, you can pinpoint why a competitor might be ranking higher on search engine result pages.

    It reveals content gaps, highlights whether your article is more or less readable than the competition, and provides keyword overlap tags to help you cover the topical cluster more comprehensively.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Paste Content: Paste your article draft or HTML page into the left text box (Content A) and your competitor's content into the right text box (Content B).
    2. Review the Comparison Table: Check the automated advantage indicators for word count, readability ease, heading hierarchy, and image counts.
    3. Examine Keyword Coverage: Compare the top recurring phrases in both pieces to uncover secondary keywords or subtopics your competitor covers that you missed.
    4. Swap or Adjust: Click Swap Texts to toggle perspectives, or edit your text directly in the box to watch your metrics improve live.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • Dual DOM Parsing: Processes both inputs simultaneously using isolated browser DOMParser instances.
    • Readability Differential: Computes comparative Flesch Reading Ease scores and identifies target reading grade differentials.
    • Topical Extraction: Strips English stop words to extract genuine content keywords and display frequency badges.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Due to browser CORS policies, this tool does not crawl live external URLs directly. To compare a live competitor page, view the page source, copy the HTML or article text, and paste it into the Competitor input box.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    All side-by-side parsing and comparative statistics are computed locally in your browser tab. Neither your private drafts nor your competitor research notes are transmitted to an external server.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does having more words guarantee a higher Google rank?

    Not necessarily. While comprehensive content frequently outperforms thin content, Google values relevance, helpfulness, and user satisfaction over pure word count. Having a clear structure and concise answers is essential.

    What does the "Advantage" column indicate?

    It highlights which document meets best practices for a specific metric (for instance, higher word count, superior readability ease, or better heading distribution).

    Can I paste raw text instead of HTML?

    Yes. The tool automatically handles plain text as well as HTML markup. If plain text is provided, word and readability metrics will calculate normally while HTML heading counts remain at zero.
