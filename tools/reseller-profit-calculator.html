The Reseller's Dilemma: Why Gross Sales Don't Equal Real Profit

In the reselling and thrifting community, it is common to celebrate gross sales revenue: "I made \$500 this weekend on Poshmark!" However, experienced e-commerce sellers know that gross sales figures can be dangerously deceptive. Between marketplace commissions, shipping label subsidizations, packaging overhead, and thrift purchase costs (COGS), actual net profit frequently lands 30% to 50% below top-line revenue.

Calculating every expense upfront prevents the common trap of working dozens of hours packaging and shipping inventory only to break even or lose money.

2026 Marketplace Fee Structures Compared: Poshmark vs. Mercari vs. Vinted vs. eBay vs. Depop

Each leading secondary marketplace operates a distinct fee schedule and buyer demographic:

  • Poshmark: Charges a simple flat \$2.95 fee on orders under \$15.00, or a flat 20% commission on orders \$15.00 and above. Poshmark provides an expedited 5-lb USPS Priority Mail label, but seller-offered shipping discounts (mandatory for Closet Clear Out and offer-to-likers) come straight out of seller profit.
  • Mercari: Charges a flat 10% selling fee upon item completion. Sellers can have buyers pay for shipping or offer free shipping using Mercari's competitive prepaid label weight tiers.
  • Vinted: The lowest fee platform for sellers: 0% selling fee. Vinted charges buyers a Buyer Protection fee (5% + \$0.70). Sellers keep 100% of their listed item price, minus optional paid promotions like Bumps or Wardrobe Spotlights.
  • eBay: Charges a standard 13.25% + \$0.30 Final Value Fee across most clothing, shoes, and accessories categories. Note that eBay calculates this fee on the total order amount, including any shipping fees paid by the buyer.
  • Depop: Features 0% selling commission with standard payment processing (3.3% + \$0.45). Sellers can optionally enable an 8% Boost fee to appear higher in search algorithms.

Demystifying Poshmark's \$15 Threshold Rule (\$2.95 vs 20%)

Many sellers do not realize that Poshmark's \$2.95 flat fee on items under \$15.00 actually represents an effective commission rate much higher than 20%:

  • Selling an item for \$15.00 incurs a 20% fee = \$3.00 (You keep \$12.00).
  • Selling an item for \$10.00 incurs a \$2.95 flat fee = 29.5% effective commission (You keep \$7.05).
  • Selling an item for \$7.00 incurs a \$2.95 flat fee = 42.1% effective commission (You keep \$4.05).

Because of this cliff, reselling clothing under \$15.00 individually on Poshmark is rarely economical; bundling multiple lower-cost items into a single \$25+ listing yields substantially higher net margins.

Sourcing Economics: Return on Investment (ROI) vs. Net Profit Margin

Two key metrics dictate sustainable inventory sourcing: 

Return on Investment (ROI %) = (Net Profit / Total Invested Cost) * 100
Net Profit Margin % = (Net Profit / Gross Sale Price) * 100

If you purchase a vintage band tee for \$4 at Goodwill, spend \$0.50 on a poly mailer, and sell it on Mercari for \$40:

  • Platform Fee (10%): \$4.00
  • Net Payout: \$36.00
  • Net Profit: \$36.00 - \$4.50 = \$31.50
  • ROI: 700% (\$31.50 profit on \$4.50 invested)
  • Margin: 78.8% (\$31.50 kept out of \$40.00 sale)

Shipping Strategies: Free Shipping vs. Buyer-Paid Labels

While buyers love the psychology of "Free Shipping," offering it without adjusting your listing price directly cannibalizes profits. For lightweight clothing under 8 ounces (e.g. swimwear, t-shirts), offering free shipping with a \$4.30 USPS Ground Advantage label and raising the price by \$5 often increases conversion. For heavy winter coats or footwear (over 2 lbs), having buyers pay actual calculated postage or utilizing Poshmark's standard 5-lb flat-rate label is significantly safer.

The Real Impact of Promoted and Boosted Listing Fees

Marketplaces increasingly monetize sellers by pushing pay-to-play visibility:

  • eBay Promoted Listings: Allows bidding between 2% and 15%+ ad fee. This fee is only charged if the buyer clicks the ad and buys within 30 days.
  • Depop Boosted Listings: Flat 8% surcharge on sales originating from boosted search placements.

On a \$50 Depop sale, enabling Boost raises total deductions from \$2.10 to \$6.10, cutting your profit margin by 8 full percentage points. Use our calculator to evaluate whether promotional boosts leave enough profit to justify the extra visibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Poshmark calculate seller commission fees?

Poshmark applies a two-tiered fee structure: for sales under $15.00, Poshmark charges a flat commission of $2.95. For sales of $15.00 and above, Poshmark deducts a flat 20% commission from the listing sale price. Any seller-offered shipping discounts (e.g. $4.99 or $5.95) are deducted directly from the seller's net payout.

What is the difference between ROI % and Net Profit Margin % for resellers?

Return on Investment (ROI %) measures your financial multiplier on the capital spent to source and ship an item: (Net Profit / Total Invested Cost) * 100. Profit Margin % measures what fraction of the final sale price you keep as take-home cash: (Net Profit / Sale Price) * 100. A $5 thrift haul sold for $40 yields an impressive 350% ROI but a 50% profit margin after marketplace fees.

Which reselling platform has the lowest seller fees in 2026?

Vinted charges 0% seller commission fees (buyers pay an optional Buyer Protection fee). Depop charges 0% selling commission with standard payment processing (3.3% + $0.45). Mercari charges a 10% flat selling commission. eBay charges 13.25% + $0.30 final value fee on total order value. Poshmark charges 20% on sales over $15.

How does the reverse target price calculator help when thrift sourcing?

When sourcing inventory at Goodwill or estate sales, sellers often know their buy cost (e.g. $8) and their target net profit goal (e.g. $20). The reverse calculator determines the exact minimum listing price needed on each app after accounting for non-negotiable commissions, shipping discounts, and poly mailer costs.
