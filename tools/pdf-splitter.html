    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The PDF Splitter separates large PDF documents into smaller, targeted files by extracting user-selected page ranges. It allows you to isolate a specific chapter from a book, pull out a single invoice from a batch, or separate contracts without installing desktop software.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Select or drag and drop a PDF file into the upload zone.
    2. Enter your desired page range using 1-based page numbers (e.g., 1-3, 5, or 8-12).
    3. Click "Split PDF" to extract the pages.
    4. Your split PDF document downloads automatically.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • Legal Contract Separation: Extract specific signed signature pages or addenda from a 50-page master agreement.
    • Textbook Chapter Extraction: Split a large 600-page course textbook into individual chapter files for lighter reading on mobile devices.
    • Extracting Invoice Sheets: Pull individual customer billing pages out of monthly batch statements.

    Realistic Example & Expected Output

    Example Input
    10-page document, Range: "2-4, 7"
    Expected Output
    New 4-page PDF containing original pages 2, 3, 4, and 7.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Uses pdf-lib's copyPages() method. Page numbering is strictly 1-based in the UI, mapping to 0-based internal arrays. Out-of-bounds selections are validated and rejected with descriptive error guidance.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    The output document must contain at least 1 valid page. Encrypted or password-protected PDFs are not supported.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    100% client-side memory execution. Your confidential contracts and papers never leave your device.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are page numbers 1-based or 0-based?

    Page numbering is strictly 1-based, matching the physical page numbers printed on your document.

    Can I specify non-consecutive page ranges?

    Yes. You can combine ranges and single pages using commas, such as "1-3, 5, 8-10".

    Will the original PDF file be changed or deleted?

    No. Your original file remains untouched on your computer; a new split PDF is generated and downloaded.
