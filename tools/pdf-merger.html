    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The PDF Merger combines multiple independent PDF documents into a single consolidated file directly in your web browser. It solves the common problem of assembling multi-file job applications, combining monthly receipts for accounting, or collating report chapters without uploading confidential records to third-party servers.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Click "Choose PDF Files" or drag and drop two or more PDF files into the upload box.
    2. Reorder your files in the list using the "Move Up" and "Move Down" buttons, or delete unwanted files.
    3. Click the "Merge PDFs" button to initiate local client-side processing.
    4. The combined document is generated and downloaded directly to your device.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • Job Applications & Academic Submissions: Merge your cover letter, resume, and letters of recommendation into a single cohesive PDF packet.
    • Accounting & Expense Reports: Combine monthly vendor invoices, flight tickets, and receipt scans into a single audit file for tax filing.
    • Report Collation & E-Books: Merge separate chapters, title pages, and appendices into a complete publication document.

    Realistic Example & Expected Output

    Example Input

    Expected Output

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Powered by self-hosted pdf-lib v1.17.1 (MIT licensed). Binary streams are read into browser memory as ArrayBuffer objects, copied page-by-page into a new PDFDocument, and serialized to a downloadable Blob URL.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Maximum document volume is constrained by your device RAM. Encrypted or password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before merging. The tool does not apply lossy image re-compression to the output.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    Complete privacy guaranteed: your files never leave your computer. All merging takes place on your local CPU. No server uploads, no network tracking, and no file retention.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

    You can merge dozens of files at once, bounded only by your browser available RAM.

    Does merging reduce PDF quality?

    No. The merger copies vector page streams and embedded images losslessly without re-compressing them.

    Can I reorder the files before merging?

    Yes. Use the up and down arrows in the file queue to arrange the documents in your exact preferred sequence.

    Does this tool work on mobile devices?

    Yes. It runs smoothly on modern mobile and tablet browsers with sufficient memory.
