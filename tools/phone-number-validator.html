    What Does the Phone Number Validator Do?

    The Phone Number Validator & Format Checker validates the structural syntax of international telephone numbers against the ITU-T E.164 standard. The E.164 standard defines the international numbering plan used in telecommunications, ensuring that numbers are globally unique, routable, and adhere to a maximum length of 15 digits (excluding the international prefix).

    This utility parses country calling codes (e.g., +1 for North America, +44 for the United Kingdom, +91 for India), cleans away inconsistent punctuation, spaces, and dashes, and generates standardized dialable strings including tel: URIs for mobile web links.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the E.164 format?

    E.164 is an international standard numbering plan established by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). An E.164 number begins with a plus sign (+), followed by the country calling code, national destination code (area code), and subscriber number, containing no spaces, dashes, or parentheses, and capped at 15 digits.

    Why must I include the country code?

    Without an international prefix (like +1 or +44), a 10-digit number is ambiguous because multiple countries utilize 10-digit national telephone numbers.

    Can this tool detect whether a phone is a mobile or landline?

    Determining whether a specific number is mobile, VoIP, or landline requires live carrier routing lookups (HLR). Since this tool runs 100% offline in your browser, it provides structural format checks only.
