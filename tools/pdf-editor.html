    What Does the PDF Editor Do?

    The PDF Editor & Annotator provides focused, client-side document markup tools. You can overlay custom text stamps (such as "APPROVED", "DRAFT", or invoice notes), apply blackout redaction boxes to permanently obscure confidential numbers, or highlight key clauses directly on any page of an existing PDF.

    Powered by pdf-lib, all edits are composited directly into the PDF structure in your browser memory. This guarantees that confidential contracts, financial audits, and personal tax forms remain completely private and are never transmitted to cloud servers.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Open PDF: Select or drag your PDF document into the dropzone.
    2. Select Page: Use the page dropdown to choose which page of the document you wish to mark up.
    3. Add Text or Redaction: Configure your text message (size and color) or enter redaction box dimensions, then click Apply.
    4. Save & Download: Review your staged annotations in the queue and click Save & Download Edited PDF.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • Engine: Direct PDF manipulation via pdf-lib (MIT License) running client-side.
    • Coordinate Transformation: Automatically translates top-down web coordinates into PDF Standard bottom-left Cartesian coordinates.
    • Standard Fonts: Embeds standard Type 1 Helvetica font dictionaries ensuring crisp text rendering in all PDF readers.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    This tool is designed for adding overlay text, blackout boxes, and annotations. It does not re-flow or modify the original underlying typography of scanned or pre-existing vector text streams. Redaction rectangles draw opaque solid black vector boxes directly over the target coordinates.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    Your PDF documents and sensitive legal records are never uploaded to any cloud server. Every modification and export runs strictly inside your local web browser session.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How are coordinates measured?

    Positions are measured in standard PDF typographical points (where 72 points equals 1 inch). A standard A4 page is approximately 595 × 842 points; a standard US Letter page is 612 × 792 points.

    Can someone remove the blackout redaction box?

    The blackout box is saved directly as a permanent opaque drawing operator on the page stream. In modern PDF viewers, the visual pixels beneath the rectangle are obscured by the solid black fill.

    Can I add multiple annotations to different pages?

    Yes. You can switch between pages using the dropdown, add annotations to each page, and save all changes simultaneously into the downloaded PDF.
