    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The PDF Page Rotator corrects the orientation of sideways or upside-down pages in a PDF. Whether you scanned a landscape spreadsheet, an upside-down form, or mobile scanner pages, this tool rotates target pages in 90-degree increments and preserves the rest.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Upload your PDF document.
    2. Select the rotation angle: 90° Clockwise, 180° Flip, or 270° Counter-Clockwise.
    3. Choose which pages to rotate: All Pages, Odd Pages Only, Even Pages Only, or a Custom Page List (e.g. 2, 4).
    4. Click "Rotate Pages & Download" to retrieve your correctly oriented PDF.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • Correcting Scanned Invoices: Fix upside-down pages caused by misaligned scanner feeding.
    • Orienting Landscape Spreadsheets: Rotate wide architectural drawings, financial sheets, or charts so they can be read comfortably on desktop monitors.
    • Duplex Scanner Error Correction: Rotate only even-numbered pages that were scanned upside-down during two-sided document scanning.

    Realistic Example & Expected Output

    Example Input
    3-page document where page 2 is sideways. Target: Page 2, Rotation: 90° Clockwise
    Expected Output
    Document with page 2 rotated 90° and pages 1 & 3 preserved untouched.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Mutates the /Rotate attribute in target page dictionary objects using pdf-lib's degrees() method: $\text{NewAngle} = (\text{CurrentAngle} + \theta) \pmod{360}$. Non-targeted pages are left untouched.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Rotations must be multiples of 90 degrees (90°, 180°, 270°) to conform to ISO 32000 PDF standards; arbitrary angle shearing (e.g. 15°) is not supported by standard PDF page dictionaries.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    100% client-side memory processing. Zero server transmission.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I rotate just one page in a multi-page document?

    Yes. Select "Custom Pages" and enter the specific page number you wish to rotate.

    Does rotating degrade the quality of text or images?

    No. Rotating a PDF updates a metadata orientation tag; it does not re-compress or degrade the embedded content.

    What is the difference between 90° and 270° rotation?

    90° rotates clockwise (to the right), while 270° rotates counter-clockwise (to the left).
