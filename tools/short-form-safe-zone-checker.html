What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

Short-form video algorithms across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts favor videos that capture viewer attention immediately in the first 2 seconds. However, each platform layers heavy translucent UI elements (profile avatars, usernames, like counts, comment dialogs, sound marques, and search tabs) directly on top of your video.

If your speech captions, subtitle graphics, punchlines, or key product shots are placed within these overlay zones, they get covered by buttons, causing viewers to miss key information and swipe away. This tool lets you simulate and preview your 9:16 footage against authentic UI HUDs and export precise 1080×1920 transparent PNG templates for your video editor.

How to Use Safe-Zone Templates in CapCut, Premiere Pro & DaVinci

Using the downloaded 1080×1920 transparent PNG guide simplifies video editing across all major desktop and mobile NLEs:

  • CapCut (Desktop & Mobile): Import the transparent PNG overlay into your media pool. Drag it onto the highest video track (Track 2 or Overlay track) and stretch it across the entire timeline length. Align your text, titles, and stickers inside the green safe boundary, then mute or delete the guide track before exporting.
  • Adobe Premiere Pro: Place the safe zone PNG on Video Track 3 above your footage and subtitles. Set the track visibility toggle to Eye so you can turn the guide on and off instantly with one click.
  • DaVinci Resolve: Add the overlay as an upper track in the Edit page. You can lock the overlay track to prevent accidental moves while adjusting subtitle transforms in the Inspector.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the safest place to put captions on short-form videos?

The safest place for captions and subtitles is in the vertical center band (between Y: 700px and Y: 1400px from the top) and horizontally centered with at least 80px margins on both sides. This ensures complete visibility on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts without touching any UI button or description.

Why is the Instagram Reels safe zone different from the profile grid?

Reels play in full-screen 9:16 (1080×1920), but when a follower browses your main profile tab, Instagram crops the thumbnail to 4:5 (1080×1350) or 1:1 (1080×1080). This tool displays the yellow 4:5 guide box so you can verify that cover typography won't be chopped in half on your grid.

Can I upload horizontal 16:9 video footage?

Yes. You can toggle between "Cover (Crop to 9:16)" and "Contain (Letterbox)" to check how horizontal footage appears when repurposed into a vertical short.
