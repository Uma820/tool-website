    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The Reading Time Estimator calculates how long an audience will take to read or listen to a piece of content. It helps content creators place informative "5 min read" badges on blog posts and allows keynote speakers to calibrate their timing before taking the stage.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Paste your draft or manuscript into the editor.
    2. Adjust the Words-Per-Minute (WPM) speed slider or select presets (150 WPM Slow, 225 WPM Average, 300 WPM Fast).
    3. View the estimated Reading Time, Speaking Time (at 130 WPM), and total word/character metrics.
    4. Copy the calculated read-time badge text directly.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • Blog Post Read-Time Badges: Add reader-friendly time estimates (e.g. "4 min read") to articles on Medium, Substack, or personal blogs.
    • Conference Speech Timing: Ensure your 15-minute conference presentation does not exceed speaking limits (approximately 1,950 words).
    • Curriculum & Reading Assignments: Help educators estimate how much time students need to complete assigned textbook readings.

    Realistic Example & Expected Output

    Example Input
    A 900-word article draft evaluated at 225 words per minute.
    Expected Output
    Reading Time: 4 minutes (4m 0s) | Speaking Time: 6 minutes 55 seconds (at 130 WPM)

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Calculates duration via the verified formula: Time (Minutes) = Total Words / WPM. Seconds are computed via modulo math. Total words are extracted using boundary tokenization (/\s+/).

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Labeled strictly as an estimate. Complex academic, legal, or technical documentation naturally requires slower comprehension speeds than conversational prose.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    100% in-browser processing. No manuscript content is ever transmitted.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the standard silent reading speed for adults?

    Scientific studies establish average adult silent reading speeds between 200 and 250 words per minute.

    How fast does an average person speak during a presentation?

    Conversational public speaking typically ranges between 120 and 140 words per minute; 130 WPM is our benchmark.

    Can I customize the WPM rate?

    Yes. You can edit the WPM input or select from Slow, Average, and Fast presets.
