    What Does the Grade Calculator Do?

    The Grade Calculator & Final Exam Target computes weighted course grades across multiple homework assignments, quizzes, lab reports, midterm exams, and projects. Most high schools, colleges, and universities weight different components unequally (for example, homework might be 20%, midterms 30%, and the final exam 50%).

    In addition to calculating your current weighted percentage, 4.0 GPA equivalent, and letter grade, it includes an intelligent Final Exam Target Calculator that calculates the exact percentage score you must achieve on your final test to reach an A, B, or passing grade.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Add Your Course Work: Click + Add Assignment to create rows for each syllabus category (e.g., Homework, Midterm 1, Term Paper), or load the Sample Course.
    2. Input Grades & Weights: Enter the percentage grade you received (or score out of 100) and the category weight assigned in your course syllabus.
    3. Inspect Overall Standing: Review the primary cards for your current weighted average, letter grade (A+, A, B, etc.), and 4.0 GPA equivalent.
    4. Solve for Final Exam: Enter your desired overall course grade and the final exam's weight percentage to calculate the minimum exam score required.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • Weighted Formula: Current Grade = Σ(Grade_i × Weight_i) / Σ(Weight_i).
    • Final Exam Formula: Required Score = (Desired Grade - Current Weighted Points) / Final Weight.
    • Standard Scale: Maps percentages to standard North American grades (93%+ = A, 90-92% = A-, 87-89% = B+, 83-86% = B, etc.).

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Grading policies vary by institution and professor. Some instructors employ curved distributions, drop lowest scores, or use unweighted total points systems. If your course uses curve adjustments or drops your lowest quiz, adjust your entered values accordingly.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What if my syllabus weights don't add up to 100% yet?

    The calculator automatically calculates your current grade based on the weight completed so far. If you have only finished 60% of the class, it normalizes your score to 100% so you know your exact standing.

    What does a negative required final exam score mean?

    If the final exam solver displays a negative number or 0%, congratulations! You have already earned enough points that you are mathematically guaranteed your target grade even with a 0% on the final.

    What if the required final exam score is over 100%?

    If the required score exceeds 100%, your target grade is mathematically impossible without extra credit or a grading curve.
