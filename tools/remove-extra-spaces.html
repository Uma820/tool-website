    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The Remove Extra Spaces tool normalizes erratic whitespace in text pasted from PDFs, OCR scanners, legacy documents, or formatted spreadsheets. It removes accidental double spaces, strips leading and trailing spaces from individual lines, and turns tab characters into standard spaces.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Paste your unformatted or spacing-damaged text into the input textarea.
    2. Check or uncheck the formatting options: "Collapse consecutive spaces", "Trim line beginnings and ends", and "Convert tabs to spaces".
    3. Click "Clean Spaces" to apply the sanitization rules.
    4. Click "Copy Output" to copy your clean, normalized text to the clipboard.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • PDF & OCR Extraction Cleanup: Fix erratic gaps, double-spaces, and broken column alignments that occur when copying text from PDF documents or OCR scans.
    • Source Code & Data Preparation: Sanitize tab-delimited records and eliminate trailing whitespace from CSV data before database ingestion.
    • Typesetting & Copywriting: Eliminate obsolete double-spacing after periods to adhere to modern typography and editorial standards.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Processes text using regular expression token replacement: /[^\S\r\n]+/g collapses consecutive non-newline whitespace into single spaces. Line trimming applies /^[ \t]+|[ \t]+$/gm, preserving newline structure while cleaning line margins.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    This tool preserves intentional line breaks (\n). To eliminate completely blank lines between paragraphs, use the Remove Empty Lines tool.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    Whitespace normalization runs strictly on your device CPU. Your text never leaves your browser.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does this tool remove line breaks?

    No. Line breaks are preserved so paragraph structure remains intact. Only horizontal whitespace (spaces and tabs) is normalized.

    Can I convert tabs into a custom number of spaces?

    Yes. The tab conversion feature expands tab characters into standard 4-space indentations.

    Is it safe to paste confidential documents here?

    Yes. All processing is 100% client-side with zero network requests or tracking.
