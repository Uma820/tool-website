    The Universal Unit Converter provides instant conversion between metric, imperial, and digital measurement units. It eliminates guesswork for international cooking, engineering calculations, travel distances, and digital file sizes.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Uses a base-unit normalization architecture (e.g., all lengths normalize to meters, then convert to the target unit). Temperature uses standard affine transformations: $C = (F - 32) \times \frac{5}{9}$.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Uses standard international scientific definitions. Specialized regional non-standard trade units (e.g. historical apothecary ounces) are not included.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How many kilometers are in a mile?

    One mile is equal to exactly 1.609344 kilometers (1 km ≈ 0.621371 miles).

    How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

    Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 (or 1.8) and add 32: F = (C * 1.8) + 32.

    Are digital storage conversions decimal or binary?

    Standard storage calculations use decimal units (1 GB = 1,000 MB) aligned with modern operating systems and storage drive manufacturers.
