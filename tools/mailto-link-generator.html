    User Problem & Purpose

    Creating an email link on a website or in a marketing newsletter can be frustrating when dealing with spaces, linebreaks, question marks, and multiple recipients. Hand-writing URL-encoded parameters frequently results in broken links. This generator formats all parameters adhering strictly to the official RFC 6068 standard.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Enter your primary recipient email address in the Recipient Email (To) box.
    2. Optionally provide carbon copy (CC) and blind carbon copy (BCC) addresses.
    3. Specify the default subject line so incoming messages can be automatically filtered by your email rules.
    4. Type your pre-filled draft body text. Line breaks will be preserved cleanly.
    5. Copy the raw link, the complete HTML <a> tag, or the Markdown snippet for your repository or newsletter.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • Website Contact Buttons: Adding click-to-email links on portfolio sites, footers, and landing pages.
    • Job Application Postings: Pre-filling the exact job reference number and applicant name placeholder in the subject line.
    • Bug Reports & Feature Requests: Pre-formatting diagnostic questions (e.g. "Browser: / OS: / Steps to reproduce:") into the email body.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    The mailto: URI scheme is specified by RFC 6068. Query headers begin with a question mark (?) and are chained using ampersands (&). Reserved characters including spaces (%20), line feeds (%0A), and quotation marks are encoded using standard percent-encoding to ensure compatibility across all operating systems.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    When a user clicks a mailto: link, the behavior depends on the visitor's operating system default email application. If the visitor does not have an email client configured (e.g. using webmail without handler permissions), clicking the link may prompt them to choose an app.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    All URL encoding is computed locally in your browser. We never collect or store the email addresses or draft messages you enter into this tool.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I send emails to multiple people at once?

    Yes. You can enter multiple comma-separated email addresses in the To, CC, or BCC fields (e.g. alice@example.com, bob@example.com).

    How do linebreaks work in the email body?

    Linebreaks are automatically converted to %0A in the mailto URL, ensuring that newlines appear correctly formatted when the email composer opens.

    Will this link work on mobile devices?

    Yes. Tapping a mailto link on an iPhone or Android smartphone automatically launches the device's native Mail or Gmail app with the recipient and draft pre-filled.
