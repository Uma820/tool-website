    What This Tool Does

    The Print Resolution & Aspect Ratio Report is designed specifically for Etsy digital download sellers who sell printable wall art. Upload your master artwork (PNG, JPEG, or WebP) and the tool instantly audits it across all 5 standard Etsy printable aspect ratios, shows you the largest print size achievable at true 300 DPI for each ratio, calculates any edge-crop needed, provides an interactive pixel-level loupe for quality inspection, and generates a ready-to-paste Etsy listing size chart.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Upload your master file — drag it onto the drop zone, click to browse, or type the pixel dimensions manually in the override fields.
    2. Read Panel A — each of the 5 ratio cards shows the largest 300 DPI print size, a crop-percentage warning (if any), and a colour-coded DPI badge.
    3. Use the Loupe (Panel B) — pan around your image at 1:1 pixel zoom to check for JPEG compression artefacts, soft edges, or banding.
    4. Copy the Listing Text (Panel C) — click "Copy Etsy Description" and paste straight into your Etsy listing's item description.

    What is the Loupe/Zoom Previewer?

    The loupe renders your image at exactly 1:1 pixel zoom — meaning one image pixel occupies exactly one screen pixel. This reveals quality issues invisible at the reduced preview scale, such as JPEG block artefacts (pixelated 8×8 blocks), soft or blurry edges from over-sharpening or low-resolution upscaling, banding in gradients, and unwanted noise. Use the ↑ ↓ ← → pan buttons to navigate to corners and edges of your artwork, then click Reset to return to the top-left origin.

    How to Write Your Etsy Listing Size Description

    Panel C generates a plain-text block that you can paste directly into your Etsy listing "Item Description" field. It lists every size your artwork supports at 300 DPI, grouped by aspect ratio with centimetre equivalents for international buyers. Update the contact line with your shop email and you are done. Well-written size descriptions reduce customer questions and negative reviews from buyers who printed at an incompatible size.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    This report evaluates aspect ratios and geometric print sizes mathematically at 300 DPI. It does not perform automated image resampling or AI upscaling. For files larger than 100 Megapixels, available system memory in mobile browsers may constrain canvas buffer allocation; desktop browsers handle massive high-resolution master art files with complete fidelity.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    Your artwork files are never uploaded to any server. All processing — including image decoding, ratio calculation, loupe rendering, and listing text generation — happens entirely within your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and the FileReader API. When you close or refresh the page, the image data is immediately discarded. We do not log, store, or transmit your files or pixel data in any form.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What aspect ratios should I use for Etsy digital downloads?

    The 5 most common aspect ratios for Etsy printable wall art are 2:3 (covers 4×6" up to 24×36"), 3:4 (covers 6×8" up to 24×32"), 4:5 (covers 4×5" up to 24×30"), ISO A-Series (A5 through A1 — popular internationally), and 11×14" (a classic US gallery size). Most successful Etsy sellers include separate files for all 5 ratios to maximise customer coverage.

    What resolution do I need for Etsy printable downloads?

    Etsy recommends a minimum of 300 DPI (dots per inch) for print-quality digital downloads. For a 16×20" print at 300 DPI you need a canvas of at least 4800×6000 pixels. For a 24×36" poster at 300 DPI you need 7200×10800 pixels. Always create your master artwork at 300 DPI or higher — you can downscale but you cannot upscale without quality loss.

    Will my image be cropped when printed at different aspect ratios?

    Yes, if your master image's aspect ratio does not exactly match a print ratio, the edges will be cropped. For example, if you upload a 4000×5000px image (4:5 ratio) and try to fit it into a 2:3 ratio frame, the top and bottom edges will be trimmed. This tool calculates the exact crop percentage for each ratio so you know in advance. A crop of less than 2% is negligible, but anything higher should be addressed by preparing separate cropped master files.
