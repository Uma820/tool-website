    What Does the EXIF Reader Do?

    The EXIF Reader & Metadata Remover decodes the Exchangeable Image File Format (EXIF) metadata embedded inside digital camera photographs. Whenever you snap a picture with a smartphone (iPhone, Android) or digital camera (Sony, Canon, Nikon), the device automatically saves technical parameters into the binary image header.

    This data includes your camera model, aperture (F-stop), shutter speed, ISO sensitivity, focal length, lens serial numbers, exact timestamp, and in many cases, precise GPS geographical coordinates showing exactly where the photo was taken. This tool allows photographers to review shooting parameters and lets privacy-minded users scrub all metadata before sharing photos publicly.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Select a Photo: Click Select Photo to choose a JPG or TIFF file from your device, or click Sample EXIF Profile to inspect a demonstration dataset.
    2. Review Exposure Settings: Inspect the primary metric cards to check camera make, model, shutter speed, aperture, and ISO.
    3. Check for GPS Privacy Risks: Look at the GPS Coordinates row to see if geographic latitude and longitude coordinates are embedded.
    4. Strip Metadata: Click Strip EXIF & Download to generate and download a fresh copy of your image with all EXIF headers permanently removed.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • Binary Parser: Direct client-side DataView binary parsing scanning JPEG APP1 (0xFFE1) markers and TIFF IFD pointer tables.
    • Endianness Awareness: Supports both Intel little-endian (II / 0x4949) and Motorola big-endian (MM / 0x4D4D) byte orders.
    • Scrubbing Engine: Re-renders pixel bitmap through Canvas 2D, producing a clean, metadata-free image buffer.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Many social media platforms (like Twitter, WhatsApp, and Facebook) automatically strip EXIF metadata when you upload images to their servers. If you download a photo from social media, its EXIF tags have likely already been erased. For full metadata inspection, use the original photo taken directly by the camera.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    Your personal photos and sensitive GPS locations are processed 100% locally on your computer inside your web browser. No photos, coordinates, or timestamps are ever sent to an external server.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do photos contain GPS coordinates?

    Modern smartphones have built-in GPS receivers. By default, camera apps embed your geographic coordinates into photo files to organize your pictures by location in your phone's photo albums.

    How does "Strip EXIF" work?

    The tool draws the photo onto a clean HTML5 canvas and re-encodes only the pure visual pixels into a fresh JPEG file. Because only pixel data is drawn, all EXIF, GPS, camera serials, and thumbnail data are completely left behind.

    Can stripped metadata ever be recovered?

    No. Once the EXIF segment is stripped from the new file, that metadata is permanently erased from the downloaded image.
