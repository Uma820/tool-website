What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

TikTok is one of the world's most dynamic video feeds, with a dense stack of visual user interface elements occupying the outer edges of the screen. When creators place subtitles, brand logos, or punchy text too low or too far to the right, native TikTok buttons render over them, ruining readability and driving retention down.

This dedicated TikTok Safe-Zone Simulator gives creators, video editors, and agency media buyers an authentic in-browser preview of organic TikTok videos and sponsored Spark Ads, complete with exportable 1080×1920 transparent PNG guides for CapCut, Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do my captions disappear behind the TikTok username?

TikTok anchors usernames and captions to the bottom-left corner with high z-index priority. If your video's baked-in subtitles sit lower than Y: 1540px (on a 1080×1920 canvas), the native username and description will sit right on top of them.

What is the difference between organic TikTok and Spark Ads safe areas?

Spark Ads and sponsored TikTok Shop videos add a large rectangular action button ("Shop Now" or "Install") directly above the username bar. This steals an additional 80 pixels of vertical space from the bottom of your video, reducing the safe height from 1320px to 1240px.

Does this tool work with vertical video recordings on mobile browsers?

Yes! You can load clips from your phone's camera roll directly in Chrome, Safari, or Edge to test safe zones on mobile without needing a desktop editor.
