    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do you calculate percentage increase?

    Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100: ((New - Old) / Old) * 100.

    How do I calculate a retail store discount?

    Multiply original price by discount percentage, divide by 100 to get savings, then subtract savings from the original price.

    Does this calculator round decimal answers?

    Results round cleanly to two decimal places for currency, while showing higher precision when necessary.
