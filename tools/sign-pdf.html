    What Does the Sign PDF Tool Do?

    The Sign PDF Online tool allows professionals, freelancers, and clients to electronically sign PDF contracts, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), invoices, and lease forms directly in the web browser.

    Instead of printing out pages, signing with a pen, and scanning them back onto your computer, you can draw a fluid signature using your mouse or touchscreen, type your name in elegant script calligraphy, or upload an existing signature PNG. The signature is burned permanently into the PDF document's vector streams using pdf-lib with zero server uploads.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Upload Your PDF: Click the dropzone to select your PDF document from your device.
    2. Create Signature: Choose between Draw (freehand signature with pen smoothing), Type Name (calligraphic font generator), or Upload (PNG image).
    3. Choose Placement: Pick the target page (e.g. Page 1, Last Page) and select a placement preset (such as Bottom Right for standard contract signature blocks).
    4. Sign and Download: Adjust the signature scale slider to your preferred size and click Sign & Download PDF.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • PDF Core: Powered by pdf-lib (MIT License) running client-side WebAssembly / JS.
    • Canvas Vector Smoothing: Signature pad captures high-resolution pointer coordinates with smooth quadratic Bézier interpolation.
    • Transparency Preservation: Signatures are embedded as 32-bit alpha PNGs so existing lines, text, and underlines behind the signature remain clearly visible.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    This tool generates standard Electronic Signatures (e-Signatures) by rasterizing and embedding your visual signature onto the PDF page. It does not provide cryptographic Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) digital certificates (Digital Signatures / Adobe Certified Document Services) which require hardware smart cards or paid identity verification authorities.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    Your confidential legal agreements and your personal signature never leave your device. All rendering, canvas manipulation, and PDF compilation happen completely in your browser tab. No document data is ever sent to an external server.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are electronic signatures legally binding?

    In most jurisdictions (including under the United States ESIGN Act and European Union eIDAS regulations), electronic signatures are legally binding for the vast majority of commercial contracts, invoices, and business agreements.

    Can I sign on my mobile phone?

    Yes! The signature pad supports touch gestures on smartphones and tablets, allowing you to sign naturally with your finger or a stylus.

    Will the signature cover up the contract text?

    Because signatures are rendered with a transparent background, document lines and text beneath your signature strokes remain fully readable.
