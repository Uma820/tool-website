    What Does the QR Code Generator Do?

    The QR Code Generator encodes digital information into 2D Quick Response matrix barcodes readable by iOS and Android smartphone camera apps. It supports structured formats including direct website hyperlinks, plain text instructions, automated Wi-Fi network credential sharing, click-to-email messages, and phone dialing.

    Unlike online QR services that wrap your destination in tracking links, require subscriptions, or disable codes after a trial period, this tool creates 100% static, permanent QR codes that encode your destination directly into the barcode matrix with zero expiration dates.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Select QR Type: Click the tab corresponding to your desired payload (Website URL, Wi-Fi Network, Text, Email, or Phone).
    2. Fill in Details: Enter your link or credentials. For Wi-Fi networks, enter your SSID and password to generate a one-tap connection code.
    3. Customize Appearance: Choose foreground and background colors to match your brand palette, and select error correction levels (High is ideal if printing on merchandise).
    4. Download: Click Download PNG for digital marketing or Download SVG for high-resolution vector print production.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • Barcode Standard: ISO/IEC 18004 QR Code specification compliant.
    • Error Correction Levels: Level L (7%), Level M (15%), Level Q (25%), Level H (30% recovery capability if the printed code is smudged or partially obscured).
    • Vector Output: Pure SVG XML generation with infinite scaling resolution for billboard and apparel printing.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Ensure high contrast between your foreground and background colors (dark foreground on light background is universally recommended). Inverted QR codes (white code on dark background) or low-contrast color combinations (such as yellow on white) may fail to scan on older phone cameras.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do these QR codes ever expire?

    No! These are static QR codes. Your raw text or link is permanently encoded into the visual black-and-white grid pattern itself. They will work forever with zero expiration dates or ongoing subscription fees.

    What is Error Correction Level?

    QR codes use Reed-Solomon error correction. Level H allows up to 30% of the QR code to be damaged, obscured, or covered by a center brand logo while still remaining 100% scanable.

    How small can I print a QR code?

    For standard printed flyers and business cards, we recommend a minimum physical print size of 2 cm × 2 cm (0.8 × 0.8 inches) to ensure reliable camera focus.
