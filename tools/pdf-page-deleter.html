    What This Tool Does & Problem It Solves

    The PDF Page Deleter removes unwanted pages, accidental blank sheets, or outdated cover pages from a PDF. It produces a cleaned document containing only the pages you want to keep, with built-in protection preventing an empty document.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Upload your PDF file.
    2. Enter the page numbers you wish to delete (e.g. 1, 4-6).
    3. Click "Delete Pages & Download" to process.
    4. Download your cleaned PDF document immediately.

    Common Practical Use Cases

    • Removing Outdated Cover Sheets: Delete obsolete fax headers or outdated title pages before distributing reports.
    • Purging Blank Scanner Pages: Eliminate accidental empty pages created by automatic document feeder scanner double-feeds.
    • Redacting Confidential Appendices: Remove internal review notes or draft appendix pages before sharing documents with outside clients.

    Realistic Example & Expected Output

    Example Input
    6-page document, Delete: "1, 6"
    Expected Output
    Cleaned 4-page PDF containing original pages 2, 3, 4, and 5.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    Calculates the set difference between all document pages and the deletion set: $\text{KeepPages} = \text{AllPages} \setminus \text{DeletePages}$. Prevents 100% page deletion to comply with ISO 32000 PDF standards requiring at least 1 page.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    Cannot delete every page in a document. Encrypted PDFs require password removal prior to processing.

    Privacy & Security Guarantee

    100% in-browser memory execution. Your sensitive documents are never uploaded to any server.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I delete multiple pages at once?

    Yes. You can enter comma-separated numbers and ranges, such as "1, 3, 7-9".

    Can I delete all pages in a PDF?

    No. A valid PDF file must contain at least one page. The tool prevents accidental total deletion.

    Are page numbers 1-based?

    Yes. All page numbers match the physical 1-based numbering shown in standard PDF viewers.
