Open-CPM vs. List-CPM vs. Flat-Rate: How Newsletter Ad Pricing Actually Works

In the early days of email marketing, sponsorships were priced purely on Total List Size (e.g. charging a flat \$25 per 1,000 subscribers). However, that outdated model heavily penalized active writers who pruned dead email addresses and rewarded bad actors who accumulated bloated, unengaged lists.

Today, top newsletter platforms like Beehiiv, Substack, ConvertKit, and SparkLoop price sponsorships on Open-CPM (Cost Per Mille Opens). Because Open-CPM is tied directly to the number of human readers who actually view an issue, it establishes a reliable baseline of guaranteed reader impressions that sponsors trust.

The Mathematical CPM Formula: How to Price Your Newsletter Ad Slots

To calculate your publication's fair market rate for any ad slot, use the standard creator pricing formula: 

Fair Ad Price = (Total Subscribers * Open Rate % / 1,000) * Niche Open-CPM * Placement Multiplier

For example, a tech newsletter with 10,000 subscribers, a 42% average open rate (4,200 unique readers), a \$60 B2B Tech Open-CPM, and a Primary Top placement (1.0x multiplier) calculates as: 

Fair Ad Price = (4,200 / 1,000) * $60 * 1.0 = $252.00 per issue

2026 Newsletter Sponsorship CPM Benchmarks by Niche

Ad rates vary dramatically depending on the purchasing power of your readership and the average contract value (ACV) of products advertised to them:

  • Finance, Crypto & Wealth (\$55 – \$85 Open-CPM): High-earning retail investors, trading platforms, credit cards, and private equity tools. Highest willingness-to-pay in the industry.
  • B2B SaaS, AI & Software Engineering (\$50 – \$75 Open-CPM): CTOs, software developers, engineering leads, and enterprise software buyers. Sponsors frequently spend thousands per customer acquisition.
  • Marketing, Startups & Creator Economy (\$35 – \$55 Open-CPM): Growth marketers, founders, agency owners, and digital tool providers. Strong conversion for SaaS productivity tools.
  • Health, Wellness, Biohacking & Fitness (\$28 – \$45 Open-CPM): Direct-to-consumer supplements, wearables, workout apps, and healthy nutrition brands.
  • General News, Culture & Lifestyle (\$18 – \$30 Open-CPM): Broad consumer demographics. Monetized through mass-market consumer subscriptions, streaming services, and retail ecommerce.

Why Dedicated Solo Blasts Command 2x to 3x Primary Placements

A standard primary sponsorship shares email real estate with your regular editorial essays, curations, and links. A Dedicated Solo Blast, by contrast, gives 100% of the email's subject line, body copy, and call-to-action buttons exclusively to the advertiser.

Because dedicated blasts risk higher subscriber churn and unsubscribes if overused, creators should apply a 2.0x to 2.5x multiplier (our tool uses 2.3x) and limit dedicated blasts to at most 1 or 2 per month.

How to Pitch and Close Sponsors on Substack and Beehiiv

Cold pitching sponsors requires speaking their language: Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) and Effective Cost Per Click (eCPC). When pitching:

  1. Lead with Verified Engagement: Never boast about total vanity subscriber counts. Lead with verified unique 30-day open rates and click rates.
  2. Anchor High, Then Close Bundles: Quote your Premium Anchor rate (+20%) for single one-off tests, and offer your standard rate as an attractive discount when they book 4 or 8 issues.
  3. Include Trackable Links & Post-Campaign Reports: Guarantee UTM tracking links and send a post-send metric screenshot within 72 hours of publication.

The Psychology of Multi-Issue Bundling: Upselling Sponsors to 4-Week and 12-Week Packages

Advertising frequency is critical. Marketing data consistently proves that readers rarely purchase on the first impression. By packaging 4-issue (10% off) or 12-issue quarterly takeovers (20% off), you give the sponsor the repeated brand touchpoints required for a successful campaign while securing guaranteed cashflow months in advance.

Measuring ROI: CTR, Sponsor Clicks, and Effective CPC Benchmarks

Newsletter advertisers evaluate email performance against LinkedIn and Google Ads. An email sponsorship delivering clicks at an Effective CPC of \$1.50 to \$3.00 in B2B or Finance is considered extraordinary value compared to LinkedIn Ads, which frequently exceed \$8 to \$15 per click. Frequently Asked Questions

Should newsletter sponsors be priced on Open-CPM or Total Subscribers?

Modern newsletter advertising is priced almost universally on Open-CPM (Cost Per Mille Opens) rather than total subscriber count. A publication with 20,000 subscribers and a 20% open rate delivers the exact same 4,000 eyeballs as an engaged list of 8,000 subscribers with a 50% open rate. Pricing on verified unique opens protects both creators and sponsors.

What is the standard CPM benchmark for newsletter sponsorships in 2026?

Across top newsletter platforms like Beehiiv and Substack, primary sponsorship benchmarks range by niche: Finance & Crypto averages $60-$80 Open-CPM; B2B SaaS, AI & Tech averages $55-$75 Open-CPM; Marketing & Creator Economy averages $40-$55 Open-CPM; Health & Fitness averages $30-$45 Open-CPM; and General Consumer News averages $18-$30 Open-CPM.

How should newsletter creators price Dedicated Solo Email Blasts?

A dedicated solo blast (where 100% of the email is devoted solely to the sponsor) is typically priced between 2.0x and 2.5x the rate of a standard primary sponsorship. Because sending an unscheduled promotional blast risks higher unsubscribe rates and reader fatigue, creators must charge a premium to compensate for audience impact.

Why should creators offer discounts on multi-issue sponsorship packages?

Offering 10% to 20% discounts for 4-issue, 8-issue, or quarterly package commitments creates a win-win: sponsors achieve the brand repetition necessary for high conversion, while creators lock in predictable recurring revenue and eliminate the administrative time required to pitch new advertisers every single week.
