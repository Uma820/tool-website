    What Does the Video Converter Do?

    The Video Converter & Compressor reformats, rescales, and downscales video clips (MP4, WebM, and MOV) entirely in your web browser. Using the HTML5 Canvas capture pipeline and the browser's native MediaRecorder API, it transcodes video frames in real-time, allowing you to downscale high-resolution 4K or 1080p camera recordings to lighter 720p or 480p formats.

    Unlike cloud conversion websites that impose queues, upload waiting times, or 100 MB subscription paywalls, this tool runs locally on your machine with 100% data privacy.

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Select Video: Choose or drag an MP4, WebM, or MOV video file into the dropzone.
    2. Choose Target Resolution: Keep original resolution or select 720p / 480p to compress the file size.
    3. Select Quality & Format: Choose your target bitrate (e.g. 4.0 Mbps) and container format (WebM or MP4).
    4. Convert & Save: Click Start Conversion & Download. The tool plays through the clip, captures the transcoded stream, and triggers an immediate download.

    Technical Specifications & Mechanics

    • Transcoding Architecture: Employs HTMLCanvasElement.captureStream() coupled with native MediaRecorder hardware encoding.
    • Supported Codecs: VP8/VP9 (WebM) and H.264/AVC (MP4) depending on browser operating system capabilities.
    • Bilinear Scaling: Downscales pixel dimensions while preserving exact original aspect ratios.

    Limitations & Browser Boundaries

    In-browser real-time transcoding processes video at playback speed while rendering frames through the canvas. For optimal stability and memory management, converting video clips under 5 minutes in duration is recommended.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does conversion play the video during transcoding?

    The browser's native captureStream() and MediaRecorder APIs record frames as the video plays through memory, guaranteeing hardware-accelerated encoding without needing heavy external WebAssembly binaries.

    Which format is more compatible: MP4 or WebM?

    MP4 is supported by almost all devices and legacy televisions. WebM provides superior compression efficiency and is the native open standard across all modern web browsers.

    Can I cancel a conversion midway?

    Yes. Pausing the video or clicking cancel will immediately stop the recording and allow you to save whatever portion was encoded up to that point.
